WELCOME TO VILLA CASAGGIORI

A family story,
with the sea in view.

The peace of the countryside meets the beauty of the Ligurian coast and the Portofino promontory.

TRADITION AND HOSPITALITY

A long tradition of weddings and special moments.

For many years, couples and families have chosen Villa Casaggiori to celebrate their wedding.

Our tradition of hosting receptions and ceremonies is rooted in experience, thoughtful table settings and the pleasure of sharing important moments. The farmhouse spaces, views and surrounding greenery create the setting for lasting memories.

04 / THE FARM

The richness of the land,
in the heart of Liguria.

The countryside around Villa Casaggiori reflects an agricultural tradition shaped by landscapes, crops and harvests that follow the seasons.

Among Ligurian vineyards and olive groves, our land is home to vegetables and fruit trees. Each crop brings the colours and scents of the countryside and helps preserve our bond with this area.
