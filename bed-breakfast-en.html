A retreat from everyday stress, in a peaceful and relaxing setting for those who love the gentle tranquillity of nature.

Villa Casaggiori’s rooms and apartments are spread across three cottages set in greenery. Each home has its own private garden and open sea views: a welcoming place to relax, recharge and enjoy the tranquillity of the Ligurian hills.

Apartments and rooms with breakfast included are available for short or long stays. The Bed & Breakfast is open every day of the year.
